Graduate Environmental Consultant
GHD | Melbourne, VIC

About the role:
Join our environment and sustainability team supporting infrastructure and land development projects across Victoria.

Key responsibilities:
- Support environmental impact assessments for infrastructure projects
- Carry out field sampling and environmental monitoring
- Assist with contaminated land and risk assessment work
- Prepare technical reports and permit documentation
- Use GIS to analyse and present environmental data
- Work in multidisciplinary teams with engineers and planners

Requirements:
- Bachelor or master degree in environmental science, environmental engineering or a related field
- Understanding of environmental impact assessment
- Field sampling or laboratory experience
- GIS capability
- Strong technical report writing
- Full Australian working rights and a driver licence
