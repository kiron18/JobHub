Graduate Analyst Program 2027
Deloitte Australia | Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane

About the program:
Our Graduate Analyst Program is a two-year rotation across our Consulting and Risk Advisory practices. You will work in mixed teams on client problems in financial services, government and energy. No prior professional experience is required. We train you.

What you will do:
- Gather and organise data from client systems and public sources
- Build simple models and summaries in Excel to support senior consultants
- Write up findings in short reports and slide decks
- Sit in client workshops, take notes, and follow up on actions
- Learn one analytics tool properly (we use Power BI and Alteryx)

What we are looking for:
- A completed bachelor or master degree in any discipline, awarded within the last two years
- Australian citizenship, permanent residency, or full working rights
- Clear written English and comfort speaking in front of a room
- Evidence you can handle detail without losing the bigger picture
- Curiosity, and a track record of finishing things you start

Nice to have:
- Any exposure to Excel beyond the basics, or to SQL, Python or R
- Part-time work, volunteering, university societies or sport where you had responsibility for something

We do not expect:
- Industry experience
- A technical degree
- Knowledge of our tools before you arrive

Applications close 14 March. Assessment is a video interview, an online reasoning test, and a final round assessment centre.
