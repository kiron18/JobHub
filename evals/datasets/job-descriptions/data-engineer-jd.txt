Data Engineer
Canva | Sydney, NSW (Hybrid)

About the role:
Join our Data Platform team building the pipelines that move billions of events a day into the warehouse our analysts and ML teams depend on.

Key responsibilities:
- Build and maintain batch and streaming data pipelines
- Work with Kafka and a stream processing framework such as Flink or Spark Streaming
- Model data for downstream analytics consumption in Parquet and Avro
- Optimise SQL and warehouse performance, including partitioning and indexing
- Own ETL jobs end to end, including monitoring, alerting and data quality checks
- Maintain metadata and lineage so the platform stays auditable

Requirements:
- 2+ years in a data engineering role
- Strong SQL and Python
- Hands on experience with Kafka
- Experience with Spark or Flink
- Cloud data platform experience (GCP, AWS or Azure)
- Comfortable with CI/CD and code review
- Degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field
