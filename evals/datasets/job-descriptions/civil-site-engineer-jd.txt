Site Engineer, Infrastructure
John Holland | Sydney, NSW

About the role:
We are delivering major road and tunnel packages across NSW and need site engineers to run work fronts on the ground.

Key responsibilities:
- Supervise construction activities on site and coordinate subcontractors
- Set out works and check survey and levels
- Manage quality records, ITPs and as-built documentation
- Enforce safety standards and run toolbox talks
- Track quantities, progress claims and daily site reports
- Liaise with the project engineer and client representatives

Requirements:
- Degree in Civil Engineering
- 3+ years site experience on infrastructure or tunnelling projects
- Working knowledge of Australian construction standards
- White card and full working rights
- Willing to work shift roster, FIFO or DIDO where required
- Strong documentation and reporting discipline
