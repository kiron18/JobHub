Sustainability and ESG Reporting Analyst
Lendlease | Melbourne, VIC

About the role:
Support our corporate reporting team in producing ESG disclosures and sustainability metrics for a listed property group.

Key responsibilities:
- Prepare ESG and sustainability reporting to recognised frameworks
- Collect and validate emissions, waste and resource data across business units
- Support life cycle assessment and circular economy initiatives
- Write technical reports for internal and external audiences
- Engage stakeholders across operations, finance and risk
- Track regulatory change affecting sustainability disclosure

Requirements:
- Degree in engineering, environmental science or a related discipline
- 5+ years in sustainability, ESG or environmental reporting
- Experience with emissions accounting or life cycle assessment
- Strong technical writing skills
- Comfortable presenting to senior stakeholders
- Postgraduate qualification is well regarded
