Seed fixes this tree's shape, its fruit and flower species, and a little persona — eye color and shape, glasses, and one facial-hair-or-equivalent (moustache, beard, lashes, a bow, earrings). Day grows the branch structure toward day 90. Every application adds one leaf, all the same size. Every interview adds one piece of fruit. A streak grows flowers in the grass, saturates the canopy and lifts a warm halo behind it. Seven days since the last log and the eyes shut fully, the canopy fades toward pale, and the tree goes still — waking the moment a new application lands.
